Common Questions

Do you really come to my location?

Yes — Fletcher Detailing is a mobile operation. We bring our own water, power, and equipment to your home, office, or marina anywhere in Putnam County. Prefer to drop your vehicle off instead? That works too.

What areas do you service?

We regularly serve Palatka, East Palatka, Crescent City, Interlachen, San Mateo, Welaka, Pomona Park, Hollister, and the rest of Putnam County. Call (386) 937-9381 if you're just outside this list — we may still be able to help.

Can you detail my boat at the marina or my dock?

Absolutely. We regularly detail boats dockside along the St. Johns River and at area marinas throughout Putnam County.

How do I get a price for my vehicle, RV, or boat?

Pricing depends on size, condition, and the package you choose. Call or text (386) 937-9381 with details about your vehicle and we'll give you a straightforward quote.
